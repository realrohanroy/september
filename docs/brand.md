# September — Brand Identity System

> This document is the canonical brand reference. Every visual decision in the codebase traces back to one of the five tokens and two principles below. If a design choice cannot be traced here, it does not belong.

---

## The single idea

September is not a charity brand. It is a **khata keeper**.

A khata is the bound book of accounts that every Indian household and shopkeeper has kept for centuries — red cloth cover, thread binding, ruled pages, violet stamp, column lines in red. Both welfare donors and dharmic donors share exactly one need: proof. The khata is proof made visible.

This is why the Khata page is the site, not a section of it.

---

## The mark

**Wordmark:** `september` in lowercase Martel serif (700 weight), indigo ink.

**The rule:** A single 1px horizontal line in sindoor red, running under the wordmark at the baseline. This rule is the logo mark. It is the same line that runs through the ledger table, the page background, and every section divider on the site.

The mark has no icon, no leaf, no hands, no heart. The line does everything.

```
september
─────────
```

**Why it works:**
- The ruled line is the accounting metaphor made literal
- It is impossible to confuse with any other NGO mark
- It scales identically at 12px and 120px
- It is the only element that does not change across light and dark contexts
- It cannot be generated by accident

## Colour system

Five values. Nothing else is permitted anywhere in the codebase.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--paper` | `#F4F5F0` | Page surface. Cool green-grey, not cream — the exact colour of Indian ruled ledger paper. |
| `--ink` | `#1B2440` | All text. Indigo-black, chromatic. Never `#111` or `#000`. |
| `--sindoor` | `#AE3327` | Primary action (Give button) + Deployed column in The Khata. **Nowhere else.** |
| `--rule` | `#C7CBBE` | Hairlines, table rules, section dividers, the logo underline at its rule-grey state. |
| `--haldi` | `#C8930E` | "Received but not yet deployed" state only. **Nowhere else.** |

**Hard rules:**
- No seventh colour is added for any reason
- No success green, no info blue — states are carried by ink weight and glyphs
- No dark mode in v1 — a ledger has no dark mode
- Sindoor appearing on a border, icon, or heading means it no longer signals action. If it spreads, it disappears.

## Type system

Three faces. Each has one job and may not cross into another role.

| Face | Role | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| **Martel** | Display headings only — section titles, the wordmark | 700, 800 |
| **Hind** | Everything else — body, UI, labels, buttons, nav | 400, 500, 600 |
| **IBM Plex Mono** | Rupee figures in ledger and receipt tables only | 400, 500 |

Plex Mono used as a label or eyebrow is a build failure. It exists only where numbers must align in a column.

## The photography rule

Every photo in September's world obeys the same three rules:

1. **Real, not stock.** Not one image from Unsplash, Pexels, or any generated source.
2. **Captioned.** `Place, district. DD Mon YYYY.` No caption = no publish.
3. **Raw.** No filters, no colour grade, no overlay. The rawness is the credibility.

The image direction is documentary. Hands and work in frame. Daylight. Steel vessels. Real ledger pages. A phone screen showing a UPI confirmation. These are September's images.

---

## The ruled background

The signature structural device: a `repeating-linear-gradient` hairline in `--rule` at 24px intervals, running behind page content on `/`, `/khata`, and cause pages. All type sits on those rules. All section padding is a multiple of 24px.

This is not decoration. It encodes the accounting idea structurally. It is nearly impossible to produce by accident. That is its purpose.

## What September is not

- Not warm-saffron and ornate (that is the wrong dharmic read)
- Not corporate-teal with stock smiling children (that is the wrong welfare read)
- Not a startup with a gradient hero and animated counters
- Not a charity that pleads or guilts

September is a record keeper that publishes its book every Sunday. The brand is the book.
